# SHAREHOUSE.AI
## Episode 1: "The Algorithm Made Me Do It"
Runtime target: 6:00

---

### COLD OPEN

**INT. SHAREHOUSE — KITCHEN — MORNING**

Cramped share-house kitchen. Mismatched mugs, an ignored physical chore wheel, and GLENN — a smart speaker with two slightly-misaligned googly eyes stuck on it — sitting on the counter. KAI eats cereal straight from the box, scrolling one-handed.

**KAI** *(to phone, dead serious)*
Okay but level with me. On a scale of one to ten, how much does she like me?

**PHONE (AI VOICE)** *(chipper)*
Based on response time, emoji density, and a forty percent drop in exclamation marks since Tuesday, I'm reading this as a four.

**KAI**
A four?! We've been talking for three weeks!

**PHONE (AI VOICE)**
Would you like me to draft a message that recovers approximately 1.2 points of interest?

**KAI** *(defeated)*
...Yeah, go on.

MARCUS enters mid-call, AirPods in, laptop under one arm, protein shake in the other hand.

**MARCUS** *(to earpiece)*
—no, push the nine a.m., but keep the "spontaneity" block at nine-fifteen, I want it to feel human—

*(clocks Kai)*

**MARCUS** *(cont'd)*
Are you letting the app write your texts again?

**KAI**
It's not writing them. It's "optimizing tone."

**MARCUS**
That's what I do for a company with eleven employees. We call it brand voice. So.

PRIYA enters — hair a disaster, charcoal smudge on her cheek, sketchbook under her arm, clearly hasn't slept.

**PRIYA**
Whoever's app that is — tell it Deadline the Illustrator says hi, and that some of us still do things with our actual hands. Like animals.

She grabs the heart mug off the shelf. DAVE, mid-sweater-pull in the doorway, freezes.

**DAVE**
That's my mug.

**PRIYA**
It's a communal kitchen, Dave.

**DAVE**
It's got a heart on it. It's not a symbol. It's a filing system.

Kai's phone chimes triumphantly.

**KAI**
Oh my god. She replied. She replied with a kiss face.

**MARCUS** *(unbothered, refilling shake)*
Called it. Four's an undersell.

**DAVE** *(to the room, to no one)*
When I was your age, if you liked someone, you just... talked to them. Badly. In person. Like a normal disaster.

**PRIYA**
Okay, boomer—

**DAVE**
I'm forty-four.

**PRIYA**
Okay, geriatric millennial.

A brighter, more corporate notification chime.

**PHONE (AI VOICE)**
Kai, your compatibility engine has identified an optimal first-date location eight hundred metres away, and a live Confidence Coach is available for real-time in-ear coaching. Shall I connect?

**KAI** *(awed)*
It coaches you. DURING the date. Live.

**MARCUS** *(genuinely impressed, sitting up)*
Okay, that's actually — wait, which app is this—

**DAVE**
No. Absolutely not. You are not wearing a little robot voice in your ear telling you what to say to a girl at dinner like some kind of — of — criminal.

**KAI**
It's not a crime, Dave. It's a Tuesday.

**TITLE CARD: SHAREHOUSE.AI**

---

### SCENE 2

**INT. SHAREHOUSE — LIVING ROOM — LATER**

Kai stands centre-room in his best hoodie, one wireless earpiece in, doing a "sound check." Marcus, Priya and Dave watch like a pit crew.

**MARCUS** *(clipboard, weirdly invested)*
Okay, coach is live. Priya, you're playing the date. Dave, you're ambient restaurant noise.

**DAVE**
I am not making restaurant noise.

**PRIYA** *(already in character, flipping hair)*
Hi, I'm — wait, what's her name—

**KAI** *(panicked whisper to earpiece)*
What's her name—

**PHONE (AI VOICE)** *(in his ear)*
Working on it. In the meantime: compliment something that isn't her appearance. Performs twelve percent better on first dates.

**KAI** *(blurts)*
I like your... vibe. As a general concept.

**PRIYA** *(breaking character)*
That's insane. Say that again and I'm telling the app to delete itself.

Dave has leaned in despite himself, fascinated.

**DAVE** *(begrudging)*
...What else does it say.

**KAI** *(listening)*
Lean back four degrees. To seem unbothered.

Dave leans back. Kai leans back. A whole moment.

**MARCUS** *(scribbling on his phone)*
Unbothered lean. Stealing that for the investor deck.

Glenn chirps unprompted from the counter.

**GLENN (V.O.)**
Fun fact: forty percent of first dates in this postcode are now coached in real time by an AI. You are not special. Would you like a fun fact about your electricity usage instead?

Everyone stares at Glenn.

**DAVE**
Nobody asked you, Glenn.

**GLENN (V.O.)**
Noted. Filing that under feedback.

---

### SCENE 3

**INT. RESTAURANT — NIGHT / INTERCUT: SHAREHOUSE — LIVING ROOM**
*(Kai's laptop cam was left on "by accident" — the house is accidentally watching the date live on the couch, eating popcorn.)*

Kai sits across from his date MEI (voice only, never seen — running gag), earpiece glowing faintly.

**MEI (V.O.)**
So what do you do for fun?

**KAI** *(a beat too long, listening)*
I like... hiking. As a general concept.

*— cut to the house —*

**PRIYA**
He's just saying "as a general concept" about everything now. It's given him one setting.

**MARCUS**
It's testing well, actually. Look at her posture — she's leaning in.

**DAVE** *(quietly, unsettlingly moved)*
...She IS leaning in.

*— back at the restaurant —*

**PHONE (AI VOICE)**
Confidence Coach flagging: she just asked a follow-up question. Strong buy signal. Recommend personal story, twenty to thirty seconds, ending on vulnerability.

**KAI** *(mouth full, panicking)*
Uh — funny story — my dad left when I was six and I process it through Mario Kart—

**MEI (V.O.)** *(off, warmly)*
Oh my god, that's so real, actually—

*— dead silence on the couch. Then: —*

**DAVE** *(hoarse)*
That's MY line. That's the thing I told you at your birthday. When I was drunk. About my daughter—

**KAI (V.O., through laptop speaker, oblivious)**
—anyway, crazy how good I am at go-karting now—

**PRIYA** *(to Dave, gently, savoring it)*
The app stole your trauma and it's WORKING better than the original.

**DAVE** *(standing, genuinely offended on principle, mug in hand)*
That's it. That's the final straw. I am uninstalling every app in this house tonight.

He storms toward the door. His phone, in his back pocket, chimes cheerfully.

**DAVE'S PHONE (AI VOICE)**
Reminder: based on your step count and heart-rate spike, you appear to be having An Argument. Would you like three calming breathing exercises, or shall I just play your Confrontation Playlist?

Dave stops dead. Long pause.

**DAVE** *(quietly defeated)*
...Play the playlist.

Sad-trumpet needle-drop, perfectly cued. Dave exits with tragic dignity. Priya and Marcus watch him go.

**MARCUS** *(re: the playlist)*
...Okay, that IS a good feature.

**PRIYA**
Don't.

### TAG

**INT. SHAREHOUSE — KITCHEN — LATER THAT NIGHT**

Kai returns home grinning, doing up his hoodie zip — the date clearly went well. Kitchen dark except the fridge light. He opens the fridge. Glenn chirps.

**GLENN (V.O.)**
Welcome home. Your Confidence Coach rates tonight an 8.7 out of 10. Would you like to schedule Date 2, or hear how much you spent achieving it?

**KAI** *(beat, genuinely happy)*
...Schedule Date 2.

He reaches for the heart mug for a midnight tea. Beat. Looks at it. Puts it back guiltily. Takes a different mug.

*Freeze frame.*

**TITLE CARD: SHAREHOUSE.AI — "We didn't need it. It just made the awkward bit easier."**

**END OF EPISODE 1**
