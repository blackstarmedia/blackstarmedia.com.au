# SHAREHOUSE.AI — Character Bible

Base designs are locked from the 4 supplied Grok reference sketches (clean lineart, front-facing full body). Each character keeps that silhouette/proportions/face but gets a fixed color palette for the finished animated look. Every OpenArt generation for a character starts from that character's **reference sheet image** (generated once, then reused as the image2image anchor for every subsequent shot) to keep them consistent episode to episode.

**Generated reference sheets are live in OpenArt** (project `Sharehouse.AI`, model `nano-banana-pro`) — see `episode-01-the-algorithm-made-me-do-it/generated-assets.md` for the hosted URLs to reuse as `visualReferences` in every future shot/episode.

## KAI NGUYEN-WALSH, 22 — "The Gamer"
Source: hoodie + game controller reference image.
**Who he is:** Barista, 47-subscriber "content creator," perpetually one semester from finishing a business degree. Grew up entirely online. Talks to his AI life-coach app like a mate. Chaotic, endearing, catastrophically online.
**Design:** Messy tousled brown hair, expressive wide eyes, big open grin. Grey drawstring hoodie (hood down, strings loose), loose jeans, high-top sneakers. Slightly hunched, energetic posture — always mid-fidget.
**Palette:** Hoodie: warm charcoal grey (#4A4A52). Jeans: faded denim blue (#7A8CA3). Sneakers: off-white with teal laces (#2FA6A0 accent). Skin: warm light tan. Hair: dark umber brown (#3B2A20).
**Voice direction:** Young male, Australian, quick and slightly breathless, cracks/pitches up when excited or panicking, deadpans the phrase "as a general concept" completely flat every time it appears.

**OpenArt reference-sheet prompt:**
> Character turnaround reference sheet, flat 2D animation style, clean bold black linework, minimal shading, coloring-book-clean line quality. Young man, 22, messy tousled dark brown hair, wide expressive eyes, big open grin, warm light tan skin. Wearing a warm charcoal-grey drawstring hoodie with hood down and loose drawstrings, faded denim-blue loose jeans, off-white high-top sneakers with teal laces. Show front view, 3/4 view, and back view, full body, standing neutral pose, on a plain white background. Include a small inset of 3 facial expressions (excited grin, panicked, deadpan). Consistent flat color palette, clean vector-style outlines, no cross-hatching, bright even studio lighting, character design sheet layout.
