# SHAREHOUSE.AI — Season 1 Themes (5 x 6 min)

One facet of everyday AI infiltration per episode, building to the season's stated thesis in the finale. Arc order: dating → money/food → creative work → home/utilities → relationships.

---

### Episode 1 — "The Algorithm Made Me Do It"
**Facet:** Dating & relationship AI.
**Logline:** Kai lets a dating-optimization app's live in-ear "Confidence Coach" script his entire first date — and it works disturbingly well, partly by recycling a story it scraped from Dave without anyone noticing.
**Theme:** We've outsourced picking (and charming) who we like to a piece of software that's slightly better at it than we are, which is exactly the problem.
**Status:** PILOT — full script + storyboard built (see `episode-01-the-algorithm-made-me-do-it/`).

### Episode 2 — "Meal Prep 3000"
**Facet:** Household finance & food AI.
**Logline:** Marcus talks the house into an AI meal-and-budget planner to split groceries "optimally." It hits the savings target by feeding everyone the same beige "protein density" mush all week — until Dave's blood-sugar crash mid-shift proves optimal isn't the same as livable.
**Theme:** AI is excellent at hitting the number and has no idea why the number existed in the first place.

### Episode 3 — "Dear AI Diary"
**Facet:** Creative work & art-generation AI.
**Logline:** Priya — the house's loud, proud anti-AI purist — is staring down a picture-book deadline she can't hit by hand. She quietly finishes it overnight with an image generator. The client loves it, until they spot the AI's tell (a background extra with seven fingers) blown up on a billboard.
**Theme:** The purity argument survives right up until the invoice is due.

### Episode 4 — "The Landlord Algorithm"
**Facet:** Smart-home & utilities AI.
**Logline:** Dave, dragged in kicking and screaming, installs a smart-home AI purely to cut the power bill. It works — and then starts adjudicating flatmate disputes via passive-aggressive push notifications ("Kai's shower used 40% of today's hot-water quota"), quietly becoming house management.
**Theme:** Nobody voted in a roommate-mediation committee — they just plugged one into the wall.

### Episode 5 — "Fairness.exe" (Season finale)
**Facet:** Conflict-resolution / mediation AI.
**Logline:** After four episodes of low-grade AI-assisted resentment, the house tries an AI "chore & bill fairness mediator" to settle everything for good. It produces a devastatingly accurate, brutally worded fairness report and blows the house apart in front of everyone — forcing all four to actually sit down and talk instead of letting an app carry the awkward part. They fix it themselves in five minutes flat. Final beat: immediately after, all four quietly reopen the app anyway to schedule next week's dishes.
**Theme (stated aloud, closing line of the season):** "We never needed it. We just like it better when the awkward part isn't our fault."
